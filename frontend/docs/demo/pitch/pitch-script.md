# Learn Anywhere — 60-second pitch

Six beats, ten seconds each. Real app screenshots with locally synthesized narration.

## 00–10s · 01 / THE PERSON

Learn Anywhere is for learners who want useful skills but have limited internet access. It brings practical lessons to their phone, at their own pace.
