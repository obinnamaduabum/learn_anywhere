# Learn Anywhere — four-minute video

A narrated screenshot walkthrough of the current iPhone app. Twelve chapters, twenty seconds each. This is a guide to using the app; no quiz was submitted to stage a completion screen.

## 00:00–00:20 · Learning that fits your day

**Screen:** 01-discover.png

Meet Learn Anywhere, a learning companion designed around a simple idea: useful lessons should fit into everyday life. Find a course, save its text lessons, and work through them at your own pace. This walkthrough shows the journey from discovering a topic to checking what you have learned.

## 00:20–00:40 · Start with a topic

**Screen:** 01-discover.png

Start on Discover. Browse the available courses or use search to find a topic that interests you. The course cards give you a starting point, and opening a course shows what it contains. These artificial intelligence courses use original text lessons and practical examples, with source references for further reading.

## 00:40–01:00 · See the route before you start

**Screen:** 02-course-contents.png

The course contents screen lays out the learning route. You can see the lessons and the final quiz before you begin. Open the first lesson, or jump to a topic you need today. Jumping ahead does not mark the skipped lessons as read, so reading progress stays separate from quiz completion.

## 01:00–01:20 · Save first. Learn later.

**Screen:** 07-downloads.png

Save the course while you have a connection. Once the pack is on your device, the app identifies the saved material as ready offline. That means you can return to its text lessons without downloading them again. Downloads brings together the courses and languages already saved on this device.

## 01:20–01:40 · Read a little at a time

**Screen:** 03-read-lesson.png

Inside a lesson, follow the headings and examples, then try the suggested activity. Reading time helps you plan a small learning session. Bookmark a lesson you want to revisit. The app saves reading progress, while opening or returning to a page starts at the top, giving you a clear starting point.

## 01:40–02:00 · Listen when that suits you

**Screen:** 03-read-lesson.png

Use Read aloud when listening works better for you. The reading controls include playback speed, and Profile lets you choose whether lessons start reading automatically. Listening is another way to engage with the same lesson. It does not replace checking the examples or completing the activities in your own words.

## 02:00–02:20 · A simpler view, one tap away

**Screen:** 04-explained-version.png

Need a simpler starting point? Tap the lightbulb button. The lesson changes in place to a shorter explained version, rather than opening another page. Tap again to return to the regular text. You can also choose to start with explained lessons when setting up or editing your learner profile.

## 02:20–02:40 · Keep the sources in view

**Screen:** 02-course-contents.png

The reference card keeps the lesson's sources visible without repeating a bibliography inside the reading text. Use these links when you want to explore the topic further or check its background. Saved lessons remain available offline, but opening an external website requires a connection and takes you beyond the saved lesson.

## 02:40–03:00 · Check your understanding

**Screen:** 05-final-quiz.png

When you are ready, open the final quiz. Choose an answer and continue through the questions. The app saves your answers without revealing correctness during the assessment. Submit to see your score, then use answer review to examine the correct answers and explanations. Treat the result as guidance for what to revisit.

## 03:00–03:20 · Learn in a comfortable language

**Screen:** 06-language-picker.png

Choose English, French, Portuguese, Arabic, Hausa, Yoruba, or Igbo. One preference guides the app labels and lesson language. When an available language pack is missing, the app downloads it while connected. Saved packs can then be read offline. Availability can vary by course, and Arabic uses a right to left layout.

## 03:20–03:40 · Make the app comfortable

**Screen:** 08-profile.png

Profile brings your preferences together. Choose teal light or teal dark, adjust the font size, and manage reading options. Learning material and activity are saved on your device. Synchronization sends necessary learning activity to the backend when connected, and the app does not require a learner email or password to begin.

## 03:40–04:00 · Choose. Save. Learn. Practice.

**Screen:** 01-discover.png

To get started, choose one topic that will help you today. Save the course while connected, read or listen to one lesson, and try its activity. Use the explained version whenever you need a simpler view. Return to the full lesson for detail, and take the final quiz to guide your next step.

## Recording and feature scope

Actual app screens captured on iPhone 17 on 13 October 2026. Only empty exterior black render margins were removed. Source references are described from the implemented frontend; the dedicated reference card was not captured. Quiz submission and answer review are described from the implementation; the walkthrough shows quiz entry without submitting learner answers. Offline reading refers to saved text packs, while downloading, updating, syncing, and opening external source websites require connectivity. No slide or video playback inside the app is claimed.
